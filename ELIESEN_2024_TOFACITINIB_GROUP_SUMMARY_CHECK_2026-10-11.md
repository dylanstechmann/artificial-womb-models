# Eliesen et al. 2024 Table S3 group-summary check

Cycle 244 independently recomputes the arithmetic mean and sample SD across the three placenta-labeled series at each of the ten reported perfusion times, separately for maternal and fetal concentrations. The no-placenta control series is excluded because the supplement does not state its replicate count. The derived values are in [the 20-row diagnostic CSV](config/perfusion_observations/eliesen_2024_tofacitinib_table_s3_derived_summary_cycle244.csv); the check runs only after the Cycle 243 source-cell audit passes.

At 180 minutes, the maternal Table S3 values `41.9, 33.5, 31.5` nM give 35.633333 ± 5.518454 nM, which rounds to the article's 35.6 ± 5.5 nM. The fetal values `30.0, 20.1, 24.9` nM give 25.000000 ± 4.950758 nM, while the article Results report 24.8 ± 4.7 nM. The Figure 2 caption identifies the curves as mean ± SD from three cotyledon perfusions, but the plotted data were not digitized in this check. Table S3's one-decimal displayed values imply a mean interval of 24.95–25.05 nM under ordinary rounding; the one-decimal article value 24.8 implies 24.75–24.85 nM. Those intervals do not overlap, so display rounding alone cannot reconcile the means.

This is an unresolved source-summary discrepancy. Keep the individual source values and published prose intact; do not replace either, infer which is erroneous, or use this as a transfer-model input. It does not address missing volume/removal/recovery records, assay uncertainty, or human source review. No author contact was made.

As a column-mapping control, the same supplement's antipyrine Table S2 endpoints were recomputed from its three placenta labels. Maternal `39.6, 37.4, 33.7` µg/ml gives 36.9 ± 3.0; fetal `38.0, 36.8, 35.4` gives 36.7 ± 1.3 after rounding, matching the article Results. This supports the Table S2/column mapping but does not explain or resolve the Table S3 tofacitinib discrepancy. Antipyrine values remain a diagnostic only and were not added to the candidate concentration intake; see the [Cycle 245 control receipt](artifacts/perfusion-intake-2026-10-10/eliesen-antipyrine-s2-endpoint-control-cycle245.json).
