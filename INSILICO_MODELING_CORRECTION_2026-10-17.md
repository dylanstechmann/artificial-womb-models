# In silico modeling mode: correction record (D12, 2026-10-17)

**Artificial dimensionless software program; no physiological calibration, biological prediction or machine-control output.**

## What was recommended

After the T17/T18 designs, the agent recommended "closing out" the in silico
phase: freeze the variant-signature sweep, write the measurement acquisition
specification, and move the program's critical path to the physical resource
decision. The framing treated in silico modeling as a preparatory stage whose
output would be consumed by a hardware decision.

## The owner's correction

> "We're modeling physical worlds in silico."

The correction is recorded as decision D12. In silico modeling of the physical
world is the program's working mode, not a phase to exit. This is how hard
engineering programs run: fusion devices, aircraft and chips are flown
digitally long before metal is cut, and the digital twin is where iteration
happens. The physical resource decision does not terminate the modeling — it
consumes the twin as its specification, and better twins make better
specifications. The honest gap is not "too much modeling"; it is that the
modeling so far is concept layouts plus isolated fixtures, and nothing yet
runs as one physical world.

## Consequence: the modeling ladder

Adopted as the program's modeling agenda, in rung order:

- **T19 — growth-scaling exchange module.** The fixture gap T18 itself exposed:
  a finite exchange surface whose capacity grows sub-linearly with unit size
  feeding a demand that grows with the size itself. When the capacity margin
  closes, internal service falls behind while the boundary flux keeps rising —
  the midgestation "internal delivery lags growth" failure signature,
  reproduced mechanistically as a dimensionless software world.
- **T20 — spatial internal-delivery identifiability.** Extend the
  two-compartment transport fixture into a small spatial cascade and prove the
  structural fact that boundary-plus-bulk readouts cannot distinguish served
  from unserved internal tissue.
- **T21 — integrated whole-system twin.** Couple exchange, growth scaling,
  control, sensor noise, faults and failover into one deterministic simulation
  of the full I1–I4 path under all three interface variants. This absorbs the
  variant-signature discrimination sweep: the signatures stop being predicted
  and start being produced by the running twin.
- **T22 — growth-accommodation mechanics.** Extend the Kelvin–Voigt chamber
  fixture to a wall responding to a growing load.

## What does not change

The register's conventions and stop rules are untouched: every fixture value
is an invented dimensionless software quantity; synthetic results are never
biological assays; in silico work is not conditioned on the biological gates,
which bind physical execution; no hardware purchase, remote control action or
author contact arises from these modules. The deferred work items (reviewer
recruitment per D10, the acquisition specification feeding T08) remain queued
and are not cancelled — they are downstream of a better twin, not replacements
for it.

## T19 acceptance (this task)

A `growth-scaling-model` command publishes a verified bundle containing: the
size/capacity/demand/margin geometry with an analytic crossover size; the
request/served fraction dynamics with the delivered-flux bound
`F = min(demand * request, capacity)`; cumulative internal deficit with a
balance residual; the boundary-visible detection time of shortfall against
the internally accrued deficit at that time; and a witness world that shares
the boundary exactly (identical delivered-flux and bulk-stock series) while
differing in internal tissue receipt — demonstrating that internal
distribution efficiency is invisible at the exchange boundary by
construction.
