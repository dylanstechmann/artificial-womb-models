## Decision-relevant gaps

The current candidate records do not show a traceable same-unit link among delivery, tissue use, and independent progression. They do not provide a complete starting-unit roster with losses and stopping rules, or a source-validated bridge from the early interface into later support, birth, and postnatal outcomes. The Song and other human-interface sources are bounded models or early endpoints, not full-gestation support demonstrations. Public or controlled data routes found so far do not resolve the missing cohort-to-assay links. No physical measurement facility, staff, or instrument capability has been verified in this workspace.

The review should distinguish a missing source row from an unmeasured biological function, an unavailable controlled dataset, and an engineering capability that has not been built or tested. Do not fill those gaps by joining species, stages, group labels, or proxies without source evidence.

## Comparator rationale

A transport-only or delivery-only readout cannot establish recipient use or developmental progression. The three retained allocations should therefore be compared only against source-defined records that preserve unit identity and independently assess D, M, and P, including losses and missing observations. A concordant result would make an allocation eligible for a later qualified comparison; it would not prove sufficiency. Discordance would point to the downstream function or measurement that needs attention. Current evidence cannot calculate a numeric threshold or rank the architectures.
