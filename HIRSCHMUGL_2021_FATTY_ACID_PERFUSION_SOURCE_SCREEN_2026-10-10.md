# Hirschmugl et al. 2021 fatty-acid placental-perfusion source screen

**Disposition:** nutrient-transfer mechanism comparator; no observation rows imported, no model re-fit, and no author contacted.

The [primary article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41366-021-00781-x) describes ex-vivo term human placental perfusion with stable-isotope-labeled palmitic, oleic, linoleic, and docosahexaenoic fatty acids. It reports 15 perfusions divided into lean (n=8) and obese (n=7) groups. The main clearance comparison uses 13 perfusions; one experiment from each group was excluded from that analysis because a measurement was missing at one time point. The plotted clearance values are group means ± SD at 0, 10, 20, 30, 60, and 90 minutes. A separate experiment (n=1) is shown as a model-prediction check and must not be counted as another replicate of the group comparison.

The paper is relevant to an evidence-intake contract because it separates labeled maternal fatty-acid transfer, endogenous fatty-acid release, fetal-side appearance, placental tissue uptake, and model-derived membrane/metabolic parameters. Its model figure explicitly distinguishes experimental from unmeasured states: maternal-vein concentration was experimentally measured only at 90 minutes, and the syncytiotrophoblast compartment has no experimental concentration data. The fitted parameter table is an author-reported model result, not a source-cell observation dataset. These distinctions are useful when a future source supplies a model and measurements together.

The article says that perfusate samples were collected every 30 minutes for viability measurements and that LabVIEW recorded data from the blood-gas analyzer, magnetic pumps, and pressure sensor. It also defines a tissue-uptake calculation using absolute maternal and fetal reservoir quantities at 0 and 90 minutes. The five-page supplemental PDF is now retrieved from the publisher content host and visually checked. Supplementary Table 1 gives fatty-acid mix concentrations as mean ± SD across 14 experiments; Table 2 gives quality-control summaries across 15 placentae, with several measures averaged over 90 minutes; Table 3 gives group-level maternal and pregnancy characteristics, including birth weight; and Supplementary Figure 1 shows group-level placental-uptake percentages (lean n=4, obese n=3). None supplies the underlying individual rows or an explicit run-level join. The article and supplement do not expose a machine-readable run-linked concentration table, numeric per-run reservoir-volume history, sample-removal ledger, or numeric device-log time series. Those fields are **not found in the inspected article and supplement**; this does not establish that the measurements were never made.

The publisher identifies the article as open access under CC BY 4.0. This screen records the field map only and does not reproduce its tables or digitize its plots. The supplement is preserved at `artifacts/hirschmugl-2021-fatty-acid-perfusion/supplement.pdf`; `SOURCE.json` records the publisher retrieval URL and SHA-256. The study concerns term placental exchange and metabolism. Its population table contains group-level birth-weight and ponderal-index summaries from pregnancies, but no outcome from artificial gestational support, embryo development under such support, or later health follow-up. Its results should remain separate from the Mathiesen 2020 workbook and from model fixtures.

## Screen outcome

- **Public article reports:** group-level time-course clearance summaries; group-level endogenous and labeled fetal-side release; study-level frame and methods; fitted model parameters.
- **Model/data boundary:** some plotted points are experimental, some compartments are model-predicted, and the one-off prediction-check experiment is distinct from the n=13 comparison.
- **Supplement adds:** fatty-acid mixture concentration summaries; aggregate quality-control summaries; group-level population characteristics; and a group-level placental-uptake figure.
- **Not found in the inspected article and supplement:** source-level individual trajectory rows, per-run reservoir quantities/volumes, sample-removal history, and numeric blood-gas/pump/pressure logs. The aggregate QC table is not a run-level record.
- **Supplement status:** publisher PDF retrieved, SHA-256 pinned in `artifacts/hirschmugl-2021-fatty-acid-perfusion/SOURCE.json`, five pages reviewed by text extraction and visual inspection; no table values or graph points transcribed.
- **Use:** nutrient-transfer mechanism and observation-contract comparator. Do not fit dimensional transport, infer nutrient adequacy, pool this study with other placental sources, or treat fitted model outputs as measured observations.
