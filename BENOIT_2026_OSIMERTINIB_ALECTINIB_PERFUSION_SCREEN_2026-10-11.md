## Abstract-level outcomes and limits

The abstract reports normal-term human placentas and ex-vivo cotyledon perfusion. It gives osimertinib FTR 9.5%, CI 0.41, and cotyledon accumulation 28.9%. It reports that alectinib and M4 were not detected in fetal samples, with placental-tissue accumulation of 370.7% and 178.3%, respectively. A publisher-indexed discussion excerpt gives median osimertinib CI 0.42, versus 0.41 in the PubMed abstract; the reason for this difference is unresolved, so retain both source locators rather than silently selecting one. The abstract does not report group denominators, the uncertainty statistic, metric equations, individual placenta values, concentration-by-compartment time series, sample-removal/volume histories, or analytical calibration and recovery.

These are separate analyte arms from the earlier dabrafenib/trametinib reports. Similar authorship or model names do not establish cohort overlap, shared controls, or a common unit crosswalk. Do not pool the group summaries or interpret “not detected” as a zero concentration without the source's assay limits and sampling details.
