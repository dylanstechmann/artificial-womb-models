# Cabotegravir/bictegravir perfusion access screen — 2026-10-10

## Purpose and result

A bounded screen checked whether Pencolé et al.'s 2020 human cotyledon perfusion paper could add source-level observations to the placental evidence intake. The public PubMed abstract offers aggregate transfer summaries, but the publisher full-text route required payment during this inspection. The complete article and any publisher-only supplement could not be checked, so this is an access-triage note rather than a finding that run-level data are absent.

## Sources checked

- Pencolé et al., *Placental transfer of the integrase strand inhibitors cabotegravir and bictegravir in the ex-vivo human cotyledon perfusion model*, [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32796211/) (DOI [10.1097/QAD.0000000000002637](https://doi.org/10.1097/QAD.0000000000002637)).
- PubMed's Wolters Kluwer/Ovid full-text link was opened; the public browser request returned HTTP 402 (payment required). Searches for a linked public supplement or dataset did not surface one.

## Publicly visible evidence

The abstract describes open-circuit ex vivo cotyledon perfusions lasting up to 90 minutes and reports five cabotegravir and six bictegravir experiments. It gives group median/IQR concentrations and transfer summaries. The inspected PubMed record exposes no downloadable data table or supplement link.

Because full text and supplements were not inspectable, this screen cannot establish whether individual concentration trajectories, sample-removal records, volume histories, or individual recoveries are present elsewhere. No author was contacted and no values were transcribed or inferred.

## Intake decision

Keep as a public-access triage record only. Do not mark per-run fields absent; leave them unassessed. Defer detailed intake unless a legitimate public full-text or supplement route becomes available, and continue with directly inspectable machine-readable sources.
