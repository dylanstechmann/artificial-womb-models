# GSE263528 human blastoid/endometrial assembloid metadata screen

**Screen date:** 2026-10-11  
**Status:** candidate source map; human scientific review pending.  
**Scope:** NCBI GEO Series/Sample metadata, linked BioProject summary, bounded PubMed queries, author-name disambiguation against Song et al. (2026), and Cycle 260 full-stream structure checks of all 11 GEO-listed UMI matrices. Matrix expression values were not analyzed or retained; no sequence reads or publisher paper supplement were reviewed.

## Finding

NCBI GEO describes GSE263528 as single-cell transcript profiling of blastoids, endometrial assembloids, and their co-culture. The Series lists 11 GSM sample records, each linked to one SRX experiment accession and three supplementary-file entries. Three GSM records were submitted on 2024-04-09 and eight on 2026-05-14; all list an update date of 2026-08-21. These are repository records, not a verified count of embryos, donors, independent experiments, or outcome observations.

The sample labels include day-2/day-4 co-culture names and `EnAo_H0/H2/H6` names. The metadata screen does not map these labels to embryo age, developmental stage, repeated observations of the same unit, or a common experimental denominator. The Series summary describes attachment, expansion, trophoblast differentiation, and gastrulation, but the inspected metadata does not provide per-embryo outcomes or a same-unit longitudinal crosswalk. The day labels are retained verbatim and are not translated into in-vivo developmental time.

The 11 GEO-linked SRA experiment records were searched in NCBI SRA. At retrieval, every ESummary entry reported `status=suppressed` and an empty run list. GEO's sample records separately list three supplementary entries per GSM (barcodes, features, and a UMI matrix), but their contents were not fetched. An ENA read-run report did not respond within the bounded wait and was stopped; that timeout does not establish absence. Thus there is no validated public SRA run count in this intake, and no inference is made about whether raw reads exist elsewhere or are obtainable by another route. See the [SRA status crosswalk](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/GSE263528_sra_experiment_status.csv) and [Cycle 198 receipt](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/gse263528-run-status-screen-cycle198.json).

A separate `HEAD` request to the GEO-listed UMI-matrix URL for each of the 11 GSM records returned HTTP 200 with a declared content length. This verifies route response metadata only; no matrix body, checksum, feature values, or cell counts were retrieved or inspected. The processed-matrix route is therefore reachable while NCBI's linked SRA experiment records remain suppressed, but the underlying files are not yet qualified for numerical reuse. See the [matrix HEAD crosswalk](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/GSE263528_geo_matrix_head_status.csv) and [Cycle 199 receipt](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/gse263528-geo-matrix-head-screen-cycle199.json).

The GEO-listed barcode and feature sidecars were retrieved temporarily for all 11 samples (22 files; 11,569,917 compressed bytes total). Each returned HTTP 200, matched its declared content length, and was read through gzip end-of-stream; compressed SHA-256 and row counts are recorded in the [Cycle 200 sidecar crosswalk](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/GSE263528_geo_sidecar_integrity_cycle200.csv) and [receipt](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/gse263528-geo-sidecar-integrity-screen-cycle200.json). The raw sidecars were deleted after screening. Barcode and feature line counts describe file structure only; they are not embryo, donor, independent-experiment, or outcome denominators. No identifiers or expression values were transcribed. The 11 matrix HEAD lengths total 1,171,938,176 bytes; their later full-stream check is recorded in Cycle 260 below.

Each matrix URL accepted a bounded `Range: bytes=0-65535` request (HTTP 206). Exactly 65,536 compressed bytes per file (720,896 bytes overall) were read into memory, enough to parse the Matrix Market header but not sparse entries. All 11 header dimensions matched the retained sidecar row counts (matrix rows to feature rows; columns to barcode rows), and each prefix's total length agreed with the earlier HEAD value. The header crosswalk records matrix dimensions, declared nonzero-entry counts, prefix hashes and comparisons; it does not retain the prefixes. One sample header declares 36,602 rows while the other ten declare 36,601, matching its own feature sidecar. This is a file-structure difference, not a biological comparison. At the time, prefix/header consistency had not verified the remaining bytes or full-file checksums; Cycle 260 performs a bounded structure-only full-stream check. See the [Cycle 201 dimension crosswalk](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/GSE263528_geo_matrix_prefix_dimensions_cycle201.csv) and [receipt](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/gse263528-geo-matrix-prefix-screen-cycle201.json).

**Cycle 260 full-matrix integrity check:** streamed the complete compressed body of all 11 GEO-listed UMI matrices (1,171,938,176 bytes) without retaining the files or expression values. All 11 GET responses were HTTP 200 and matched their HEAD byte lengths; each gzip stream passed CRC/end-of-stream, each Matrix Market header matched the Cycle 201 dimensions and declared nonzero count, and the body-line count matched the declared nonzero entries (314,202,222 total). SHA-256 digests are recorded per file. Only the first and last coordinate records in each matrix were parsed for bounds; every sparse value was not exhaustively validated. This establishes file-structure integrity, not expression quality, sample identity, biological replication, developmental outcome, or support-system performance. The screen checked 2,260,414 barcode rows and 402,612 feature rows, finding no duplicate keys or malformed rows. ## Relationship to Song et al. (2026)

The GEO Series record links to BioProject PRJNA1097989 but has no PubMed relation. Bounded NCBI PubMed queries for the exact Series title, GSE accession, and BioProject accession returned zero records. A separate Europe PMC API check also returned zero hits for the exact GSE accession and Series title. This is a bounded index/linkage result, not evidence that no associated manuscript or other publication exists.

An earlier initials-only comparison incorrectly treated the contributor lists as disjoint. The GEO contributor `Xinbao Zhang` and the Song et al. PubMed author `Xinwen Zhang` both reduce to `Zhang X`, but the full names differ. Europe PMC returns a Song DOI/PMID record but marks it as not in Europe PMC full text or PMC, with no Europe PMC-indexed supplement, PDF or text-mined accession. These are index metadata flags only: they do not establish that the publisher provides no supplement. The publisher full-text routes remain inaccessible in the bounded checks, so the article's data-availability statement remains unreviewed. No exact full-name match or direct sample-to-article crosswalk was found in the records inspected. Keep GSE263528 unjoined to Song et al. unless direct provenance is established; do not claim universal absence of collaboration or shared materials. See the [Cycle 202 Europe PMC receipt](artifacts/gse263528-maternal-interface-2026-10-11/europepmc-publication-linkage-screen-cycle202.json).
