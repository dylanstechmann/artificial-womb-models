# Doravirine placenta-perfusion public-source screen — 2026-10-10

## Purpose and result

Check whether the earlier Le et al. open-circuit study adds public individual perfusion records that could complement the 2022 closed-circuit source. The paper gives a useful sample schedule and starting maternal perfusate volume, but its supplementary file contains group summary figures only. It adds no per-run volume/removal history or individual observations, so no numeric observation intake or mass-balance reconstruction is justified.

## Sources inspected

- Le et al., *Placental transfer of doravirine, a recent HIV-1 NNRTI in the ex vivo human cotyledon perfusion model*, [publisher article](https://academic.oup.com/jac/article/76/9/2364/6306969) (DOI 10.1093/jac/dkab202).
- Publisher [Supplementary Data DOCX](https://oup.silverchair-cdn.com/oup/backfile/Content_public/Journal/jac/76/9/10.1093_jac_dkab202/1/dkab202_supplementary_data.docx), pinned locally as `artifacts/perfusion-intake-2026-10-10/le_2021_doravirine_supplementary_data.docx`, SHA-256 `2f03e008c21f74da108f565a92e2035b6191deb6d948ede3ab3179d9d29cf8c3`.

## Findings

The article describes open-circuit maternal-to-fetal perfusion of term human cotyledons, sample collection every 5 minutes over 90 minutes, and 1250 mL as the maternal perfusate volume used for the target dilution. Five procedures are reported as validated using the antipyrine transfer criterion. The reported concentration/transfer statistics are medians and IQRs for these procedures.

The publisher supplement has no tables. It contains three embedded figure images: S1a antipyrine concentrations and S1b doravirine concentrations, both explicitly labeled mean ± SD, and S2 fetal-to-maternal ratios. The article and supplement do not expose numeric per-run concentrations or map figure points to individual placenta labels. They contain no run-level maternal/fetal volume histories, sample-removal amounts, or individual recovery/mass-balance values. No linked raw-data file was located in the inspected source set. No author contact was made.

This is a separate study and open-circuit design from Bukkems et al. 2022; do not pool their outcomes. Neither source addresses embryo development, continuous gestation, birth, or later health.

## Intake decision

Keep as a group-figure comparator only. Preserve the sample interval and reported initial maternal volume as source context, not as a complete volume history. Do not digitize mean ± SD curves into individual observations or infer recovery/mass balance from the aggregate plots.
