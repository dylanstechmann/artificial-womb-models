# Li et al. 2026 human implantation-failure model source screen

Reviewed 2026-10-11. This is an AI-assisted, abstract-and-supplement source screen. Evidence status is candidate and human review remains pending.

## Finding

Li et al. describe a three-dimensional in-chip model pairing human blastoids or blastocysts with bioengineered endometrioid tissue. The [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41443192/) reports lower blastoid implantation capability with recurrent-implantation-failure-derived endometrioids than with fertile-control endometrioids, and says a compound screen identified candidates. These statements are retained as qualitative abstract claims only. The full article and assay-level outcome tables were not reviewed, so the endpoint definition, assay denominator, timepoint, exclusions, and sample-to-assay linkage remain unverified.

## Supplement and data routes

The publisher-hosted [supplement workbook](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0092867425012309-mmc1.xlsx) has one populated sheet. Its group headers say Ctrl n=18 and RIF n=40, with 58 sequence-labeled columns. The table contains clinical/endometrial sample characteristics. Those group-header counts are not treated as denominators for the in-chip blastoid implantation or compound-screen assays. No assay outcome records or sample-to-assay crosswalk were present in the inspected workbook. Participant-level values were not copied into the repository; the [screen receipt](artifacts/implantation-failure-model-2026-10-11/li-implantation-failure-route-and-supplement-screen-cycle194.json) records only file integrity, table structure, and bounded search results.

Europe PMC marks the record as not open access and does not flag a supplement, although the publisher-hosted workbook resolves directly. Exact title/DOI searches of BioStudies and exact title/PubMed searches of GEO returned no matches. These are limited route checks, not evidence that no other public or restricted data route exists. No author contact was made.

## Intake decision and boundary

No biological observation rows are promoted. A qualitative abstract claim and a clinical sample-characteristics workbook cannot supply an independently checkable implantation-assay effect or denominator. The model informs only early implantation/interface evidence. It does not establish placental exchange, later gestational support, birth, neonatal transition, postnatal health, or treatment efficacy.
