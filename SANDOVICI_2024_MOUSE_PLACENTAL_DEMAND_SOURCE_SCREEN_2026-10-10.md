# Sandovici et al. 2024 mouse placental nutrient-demand source screen

**Disposition:** in-utero mouse mechanism comparator; no observation rows imported, no plotted values digitized, and no author contacted.

The [primary article](https://doi.org/10.1242/dmm.050719) studies an Igf2 epiblast knockout model at mouse E13.5, E15.5 and E18.5. It combines placental morphology, fetal growth, short-endpoint tracer transfer and umbilical-artery flow. At E15.5, the authors describe reduced glucose-analogue and system-A amino-acid-analogue transfer as proportionate to reduced fetal size. At E18.5, interpretation depends on tracer, denominator and sex: the combined-sex Figure 3 reports lower glucose-analogue accumulation per fetus, a small per-gram difference, and similar transfer per mm² labyrinthine surface area; its sex-stratified Figure S4 shows a stronger female effect, including an E18.5 per-area difference. For the amino-acid analogue, Figure 4 reports lower accumulation per fetus at both ages and lower values per gram fetus and per mm² surface area at E18.5. The Discussion describes both tracers as reduced after adjustment for surface area at E18.5, which does not align cleanly with the combined-sex Figure 3 per-area result. Preserve this source-level inconsistency rather than selecting one summary. These are mouse in-utero findings, not human transfer rates or an ex-utero support result.

The [University of Cambridge repository record](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/91c18c1c-5d79-4177-b2df-4f0257f3f982) exposes the accepted article and a separate 13-page supporting-information PDF. The repository record describes CC BY 4.0; the local supporting-information file and its SHA-256 are recorded in [SOURCE.json](artifacts/sandovici-2024-mouse-placental-demand/SOURCE.json). Visual inspection and text extraction found figure legends, group/sample counts, sex-stratified plots and an ultrasound observer-variation table, but no machine-readable per-litter transfer rows. Transfer plots report averages per litter with mean±SD; no plot values were digitized. The [panel inventory](config/source_inventories/sandovici_2024_transfer_panel_inventory.csv) records the source-reported cohort structure and figure/denominator locators only, not tracer outcome values. The article states that relevant data are in the article and supplement, so this is a bounded finding about those inspected files, not a claim that data are unavailable elsewhere.

The transfer assay is a brief in-vivo endpoint at two developmental ages, not a longitudinal supply trajectory. Fetal samples are nested within litters; genotype-specific values were averaged within each litter for the transfer analyses. Keep each tracer, normalization denominator, sex analysis, developmental stage, interface measure, fetal growth and circulation distinct. A single glucose endpoint cannot validate amino-acid transfer, and transfer alone does not establish fetal receipt, downstream use or healthy development. This is an architecture inference from a mouse model, not a human physiological target or operating setpoint. The age cohorts are cross-sectional; do not turn them into same-fetus longitudinal continuity.

The source remains a qualitative/mechanism comparator. It does not establish gestation-wide human evidence, embryo support, maternal-interface sufficiency, birth or later-health outcomes. Numeric intake would require source-native per-litter rows and verified cohort linkage; these were not found in the inspected article and supplement.
