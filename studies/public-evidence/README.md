# Connecting public ectogenesis studies and simulations

Partial support of a fetus that has already developed in utero is the closest demonstrated mammalian application. Sheep studies provide weeks of observations and some transition follow-up. The question that can move now is whether support preserves development across organs and into later life. Historical human perfusion, modern fetal measurements, neonatal outcomes and early embryo studies each answer a different part of that question. There is no verified continuous human embryo-to-birth trajectory in this collection. [Partridge](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms15112) · [Usuda](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2023.1219185) · [transition cohort audit](../../ARTIFICIAL_PLACENTA_OUTCOME_INTAKE_2026-10-10.md)

Codex assembled this public-source review and ran the calculations for a personal learning project on November 19, 2026. Human scientific review is pending. Each record in the [selected source table](studies.tsv) identifies the species or system, developmental stage, source class, accessible evidence, possible use and limit. The [local index](local-index.tsv) covers all 96 JSON/CSV records then present in the repository's evidence ledger, observation cards, source inventories and perfusion collection. DOI matches in that index identify literal references. Some cards identify papers by other identifiers. [Existing evidence map](../../DOCUMENT_MAP.md)

### A new result from the lambs

Four of five supported lambs were closer to the later controls on mean brain gene-expression distance across three regions. Hippocampus and motor cortex favored the later reference in all five animals. Basal ganglia favored it in three of five. The choice of reference mattered most for lamb A. Its mean distance was 0.388759 to early controls and 0.391086 to late controls. Two of four individual early/late control pairings favored the later control for A. All four pairings favored the later control for each of B–E. This makes basal ganglia and reference selection concrete targets for an independent cohort. [Animal results](audit/ovine_animals.tsv) · [region results](audit/ovine_distances.tsv)

The source's early controls were sampled at 106–107 gestational days and late controls at 127 days. The exposed lambs were sampled after 20–22 days of support. Age, selection and sex could all contribute. Four of the five exposed lambs were female and both late controls were male. The article reports adverse events including sepsis. Terminal tissue similarity gives no individual longitudinal trajectory, functional neurological assessment or human treatment outcome. Usuda's separate inflammation cohort illustrates the cost of reporting only completers. Eight of ten supported animals completed 120 hours and three of the eight assessed had white-matter injury. The in-utero controls also sustained injury. [Cohen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-79095-7) · [Usuda et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajog.2020.04.036)

**Calculation.** The GEO deposit contains four brain regions per animal. Cohen et al. excluded the early-control cerebellum from animal 2933 for low counts. The paper's Table 1 and the deposit's group, sex and tissue fields identify H4 by inference. This audit kept all 36 libraries in its sample index and analyzed the three regions with two retained controls in each reference group, giving 27 libraries. We normalized by total assigned gene counts per library, retained genes with at least one count per million in at least four libraries, then applied log2(1 + CPM). For each supported lamb and region we averaged the absolute difference over 15,924 genes separately against each control, then gave each control and each region equal weight. These are descriptive distances. The authors used a different filter and differential-expression analysis. The 15 animal–region comparisons share five animals. [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE275228) · [analysis and receipt](audit/receipt.json)

## Connections worth pursuing

1. **Test a developmental endpoint against a genuine holdout.** The mouse length audit already identifies which group means have earlier and later comparators. The sheep result supplies a separate, later-stage challenge. Compare the sheep region ordering against an independently deposited cohort if one becomes available. Record age, exposure, sex, exclusions and later outcome independently. Pooling different animals from the same institution requires a cohort crosswalk. [Mouse holdout PR](https://github.com/dylanstechmann/artificial-womb-models/pull/1) · [ovine terminal growth intake](../../config/observations/usuda_2023_terminal_group_candidate.json)
2. **Evaluate human model predictions where human measurements exist.** The Doppler work establishes an internal participant-level baseline and documents which published curves the released equations reproduce. The independent Roberts MRI waveforms offer a volumetric-flow comparison at mapped compartments, subject to age, anatomy and measurement method. In a separate MRI study of 20 women and ten ewes, weight-normalized umbilical flow declined and extraction increased with age in the human group. The corresponding ovine associations were statistically inconclusive. Cross-species transfer needs an endpoint-specific comparison. [Human benchmark and MRI input](DOPPLER.md) · [Saini et al.](https://doi.org/10.1113/JP281002)
3. **Test where exchange models lose information.** Erlich's specimen meshes supply a spatial benchmark. The repo's human placental-perfusion records supply paired compartment and time labels, with documented ambiguities. A defensible first comparison is whether an aggregate model preserves the published ordering of transport regimes as geometry changes. Fit or score measured transfer only when the analyte, material, specimen linkage, units and clock are known. The sibling organoid project provides an idealized diffusion baseline, and the perfusion-calibration project can assess uncertainty in recorded bench flow. [Human perfusion intake](../../config/perfusion_observations/ollivier_2026_aciclovir_antipyrine_dataset.json) · [Organoid Oxygen Lab](https://github.com/dylanstechmann/organoid-oxygen-lab) · [Perfusion Calibration Lab](https://github.com/dylanstechmann/perfusion-calibration-lab)
4. **Reproduce an integrated simulation before claiming integrated support.** Eindhoven researchers demonstrated a physical manikin connected to a digital model. That is a precedent for checking timestamps, signal loss and model response across an interface. The local alarm study makes one such failure measurable. A separate public human ECG dataset offers beat annotations for a signal-quality task. It uses term recordings during labor and has its own ground truth. Model and measurement provenance should travel with every comparison. [Hybrid simulation](https://doi.org/10.1186/s42490-026-00120-z) · [annotated signals](https://physionet.org/content/adfecgdb/1.0.0/) · [synthetic benchmark](../alarm-persistence/REPORT.md)
5. **Keep the early-development question open on its own evidence.** Native human CS7 cells came from one embryo. Stem-cell-derived human models, intact mouse embryos, early primate embryos and native dunnart tissue give different comparisons. Ask whether a specific stage or lineage recurs in an independent source, then look for same-unit functional measurements spanning the proposed transition. The interval from early organogenesis to sustained fetal support is a major missing observation. [Human reference](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04158-y) · [human embryo models](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06604-5) · [monkey observation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37172563/) · [Wu mouse-model code](https://github.com/hwakwu/Wu-et-al.-2026-ciToti-EM/tree/26d350777bb543ee1ca3a26d285bf36363a695a3)

VirtualPregnancy's Apache-2.0 projects supply a population-based placenta geometry generator, a reproductive simulation library, worked examples and a fetoplacental flow library. Human fetal perfusion has a history. Westin and colleagues reported 5–12 hours in previable human fetuses in 1958. Modern human comparators include fetal imaging and neonatal discharge and follow-up cohorts. For example, Bell et al. reported survival at 22 weeks as 60 of 549 live births and 60 of 200 actively treated infants. Both fractions describe the same survivors. The FDA's 2023 panel discussed first-in-human study design. Seven recorded exact-phrase ClinicalTrials.gov searches returned zero records on the review date. Those queries can miss other terminology, registries or unreported work. We found no modern human whole-device clinical outcome cohort in the sources examined, and no public basis for a date of human availability. [Historical paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1651-2227.1958.tb07643.x) · [Bell et al.](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.23580) · [FDA record](https://www.fda.gov/media/172441/download) · [registry queries and responses](registry.json)

## Acquire, reproduce and extend

The [bibliography](bibliography/records.tsv) contains 1,040 distinct Europe PMC source/ID pairs after overlap removal. Four searches returned 334 device records, 261 early-development records, 358 human perfusion records and 94 computational records. These are discovery candidates, including reviews and commentary. The [search receipt](bibliography/receipt.json) records the exact queries, cutoff, counts and output hash. Citation chasing, program records, registries and data repositories supplied additional sources in the selected table. Unindexed or differently described studies, unreported failures and closed datasets remain coverage gaps.

From the repository root, run the following with Python 3.10 or newer. Use a new output directory for each run. The fetch step downloads the six public inputs with pinned hashes. The audit then runs offline. ENA can return rows in different orders, so its manifest pins the normalized table. Upstream records can change after retrieval. Verify a changed release against its original accession before updating the pin. The versioned software archive from the human circulation study has SHA-256 `b723a099b8c20799353ec58dee29ca92e8d7e5b00e958ac1c804bfbf9e5e95f5`. Its S2 member matches the pinned public GitHub CSV. [Inputs and retrieval URLs](inputs.json) · [results receipt](audit/receipt.json)

```sh
python studies/public-evidence/fetch_inputs.py artifacts/study-inputs
python studies/public-evidence/audit.py artifacts/study-inputs artifacts/study-audit
python studies/public-evidence/discover.py 2026-11-19 artifacts/literature-refresh
python studies/public-evidence/local_index.py
python studies/alarm-persistence/summarize.py studies/alarm-persistence/runs.csv
```

New sources enter the selected table with a primary record, material, interval, outcome and limitation. Add accessible data by version and checksum. Normalize archive identifiers, retain exclusions and keep the individual animal, participant or donor as the sampling unit. Put model-generated outputs beside the measured comparator with its units and clock. A new report from the same program warrants an overlap check before counting another replication. Human clinical claims and biological calibration require review under the project's existing evidence contract.
