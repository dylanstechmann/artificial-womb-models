# Heggarty et al. 2025 human placental transfer screen — 2026-10-11

## Result

The public Mendeley Data package contains two supplementary JPEG figures under CC BY 4.0. One figure reports the study flow and the number of placentas with transfer parameters; the other plots fetal-transfer-rate percentages for dabrafenib and trametinib. Together, the figures and related abstract provide useful endpoint-level functional-transfer evidence, but the package contains no numeric table or time-resolved concentration, volume/removal, or recovery records. Retain this as an aggregate comparator only, with cohort linkage and uncertainty definitions unresolved. No author was contacted.

## Visible observations and units

Supplementary Figure 1 is a study-flow diagram. It reports 11 dabrafenib placentas and 7 trametinib placentas with transfer parameters available, after distinct eligibility, refusal, perfusion, leakage, antipyrine, and dosage-failure counts. These are source-reported analysis denominators; they do not identify a shared donor key or establish that all other stages use the same denominator.

Supplementary Figure 2 labels its y-axis “Fetal Transfer Rates (%)” and plots 11 dabrafenib and 7 trametinib symbols, consistent with the Figure 1 denominators. The related IFPA abstract reports FTR values of 8.6 ± 3.1% for trametinib and 15.3 ± 5.4% for dabrafenib; those values match the plotted central levels to visual precision. The abstract does not specify what ± denotes, give its group denominators, define the FTR equation, or explicitly link its cohort to the Mendeley figure. The standalone image still lacks a caption defining its whiskers or providing per-placenta numeric values. No symbol values were digitized or transcribed.

The repository manifest identifies only JPEG images, not a machine-readable observation table. The related abstract supplies group-level values but no individual placenta identities, serial concentration measurements, elapsed-time points, circuit volumes, sample-removal history, recovery, or per-observation analytical uncertainty. Its normal-term frame is reported for that abstract; the exact cohort link to the JAAD figure package and the relation of its FTR denominator to the figure's analysis stages remain unresolved.

## Intake decision and limits

Keep as a human placental functional-transfer comparator that illustrates between-placenta endpoint variation and explicit analysis attrition. It cannot support a source-exact concentration trajectory, amount balance, dimensional transport fit, or the developmental-support benchmark. Do not digitize the plotted points as if they were source-reported numeric rows, infer missing perfusion histories, or pool this endpoint with other compounds or studies. The evidence is ex-vivo placental drug transfer, not embryo development, continuous gestation, birth, or later health.
