# Song et al. 2026 human post-implantation co-culture source screen

Reviewed 2026-10-11. This is an AI-assisted, abstract, supplemental-material and selected full-article source screen. Full-article text was obtained from a third-party mirror whose provenance and byte equivalence to the publisher file are unverified. Evidence remains a candidate pending human review.

## Finding

The publisher and PubMed abstracts describe a human embryo/endometrial-organoid co-culture that supports observations through day 14 post-fertilization. The abstract reports early yolk-sac, primordial-germ-cell and trophoblast features, day-9 extravillous-trophoblast emergence, and impaired progression after disruption of maternal signals including hCG signaling. The full article reports a day-14 morphology-defined survival rate of 75% (15/20) in co-culture and 37.5% (6/16) in sole-embryo controls. Its operational definition requires continued diameter expansion from d.p.f. 5 to 14 and absence of visible apoptosis signs, assessed daily. These are author-reported group summaries, not embryo-level records or a general health measure; the reported endpoint does not extend beyond day 14.

## Source-data and unit check

The publisher-hosted [supplement workbook](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S1934590925004369-mmc2.xlsx) has three visible sheets, with four populated blocks on `Sheet1`: cluster proportions (A1:F8), cluster counts (A11:F18), stage totals and printed percentages (A21:G28), and a consolidated embryo summary (A31:E35). The Cycle 195 structure screen described the sheet as one table; this cell-level review corrects that incomplete description while preserving the earlier receipt as a dated record. A [106-cell extract](artifacts/postimplantation-coculture-2026-10-11/song-mmc2-source-cell-extract-cycle203.csv) pins exact labels, values, and cell locators. All 35 values in the top proportion block match their paired counts divided by the listed cluster-count total for each label. In the stage table, 27 of 28 printed percentages match the count ratio rounded to two decimal places; cell `E23` prints `5(0.01%)`, while 5 of the derived 4,998 total corresponds to 0.10%. No value was changed. The final Epi/TE summary aggregates the counts correctly, but its Epi stage percentages match embryo-only cluster totals while the TE stage percentages match all listed cluster totals; the workbook does not explain this denominator difference. See the [Cycle 203 cell audit](artifacts/postimplantation-coculture-2026-10-11/song-mmc2-cell-audit-cycle203.json).

Exact GEO queries for the article's PubMed record returned no match. A title/topic query surfaced GSE263528. The series has no PubMed relation. An initial-only author comparison appeared to overlap at Zhang X; checking NCBI's full-name metadata resolves this to GEO contributor Xinbao Zhang versus Song-paper author Xinwen Zhang. No exact full-name match or sample-to-article crosswalk was found, so the series remains unjoined. This bounded result does not establish that no other data route exists.

The publisher supplemental PDF (MMC1; 14 pages) was retrieved and visually checked at figure pages with embryo sample counts. Figure S1B labels survival rates for co-cultured embryos (`n=20`) and sole embryos (`n=16`), assessed by morphology from d.p.f. 6 to 14; Figure S1C gives the same caption-level counts for mean diameter. Figure S1D-F additionally reports image data from three independent experiments (`n=16`), invasion direction (`n=16`), and embryo-organoid interaction rate (`n=20`). Figure S2 and S8 captions report panel-specific staining counts (2/2 or 3/3 embryos), without mapping those panels onto the Figure S1 cohorts. No plotted values were digitized. The [Cycle 204 caption receipt](artifacts/postimplantation-coculture-2026-10-11/song-mmc1-caption-outcome-screen-cycle204.json) preserves the locators and counts. The supplementary caption does not provide embryo-linked fates or time-specific attrition counts; the full-article definition and cohort caveats are recorded separately.

The article's selected full text was visually checked at PDF pages 3, 14, 20 and 21 (journal pages 59, 70, e4 and e5). The Methods defines the morphology-based survival endpoint; the Results reports 15/20 and 6/16; and the Human samples section describes morphology-based selection/exclusion without giving the number screened or excluded. The Ethics statement says culture ended at d.p.f. 14 or primitive-streak formation, but the article does not map early stopping to the reported survivor counts. A [candidate observation intake](config/observations/song_2026_postimplantation_survival_candidate.json) retains the two group percentages and their reported denominators as summaries with `not_reported` individual units; aggregate cohort-coverage records preserve the selection and window gaps. Both closure checks remain unassessed, and no individual embryo row or general viability/health claim is promoted. See the [Cycle 205 receipt](artifacts/postimplantation-coculture-2026-10-11/song-article-data-access-and-survival-cycle205.json).

## Intake decision and boundary

No individual embryo rows are promoted. The two group-level survival rates are captured in a candidate observation dataset with source-reported numerators, denominators and an author-defined morphology endpoint; screened-population and primitive-streak stopping gaps remain explicit, and human review is pending. The workbook's one printed percentage conflict and unexplained Epi/TE denominator split remain unchanged. This paper adds early-interface observations through the same day-14 boundary, not placental exchange, later gestation, birth or postnatal-health evidence.
