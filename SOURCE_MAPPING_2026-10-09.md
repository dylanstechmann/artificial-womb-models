## Question and result

Can Hiraoka's public later-endpoint, sequencing and cohort records identify the same embryos and recipients across time? Can Bondarenko's movement observations be linked to embryo-level outcome records?

**Neither source establishes the required individual links.** Hiraoka's GEO records specify pooled molecular-assay inputs, including some donor/recipient and site totals, but do not map those pools to the attachment/retention cohorts or provide later outcomes for the same units. Bondarenko's Figure 1 source tables contain no specimen identifiers, and the previously inspected Figure 6 displacement table has no embryo identifier. The joint maternal-state / later-progression question and the movement-to-outcome question remain ineligible for paired analysis.
